The mathematics of a real interlingua

One objective for all the goals

Think bigger: stop listing goals and derive them. Every goal becomes a formal condition on a few objects; all the conditions collapse into one constrained optimization; the “best system” is its optimum. The unifying punchline (§6) is that realness = minimal conditional description length: the interlingua any mind can reconstruct from the world it already shares — which is why, if it is real, Maya (or an animal, or a fresh agent) can use it out of the box. Established results are cited; conjectures are marked ⚑.

0.Objects

  • Meaning space $(\mathcal M, d_{\mathcal M})$ — a metric space (or probability space with a metric).
  • Alphabet $\Sigma = \mathrm{GF}(7)\cup\{\sqcup\}$ — the seven notes are the finite field $\mathbb F_7$, plus a word-boundary $\sqcup$. Code space $\mathcal S=\Sigma^{*}$ with metric $d_{\mathcal S}$ (Lee/edit distance; at fixed length $L$ it is the $\ell_1$ sum of ring distances on $\mathbb F_7$ — the metric of the torus $\mathbb T^{L}$).
  • Encoder $E:\mathcal M\to\mathcal S$, decoder $D:\mathcal S\to\mathcal M$, renderer $R:\mathcal S\to\mathcal P$ into a perceptual/physical space $(\mathcal P,d_{\mathcal P})$ (pitch × hue × time × intensity).
  • Channel $\Gamma:\mathcal S\to\mathcal S$ — a stochastic map (noise, motor slips, perceptual confusion).
  • Mind $m$ — a representation $\phi_m:\text{world}\to\mathcal M$ plus a policy. Two minds share the code if they use the same $(\mathcal M, E)$.

2.The unified program (all goals at once)

The best system minimises end-to-end communication failure subject to the goal-constraints:

$$\min_{E,\,D,\,R}\ \ \underbrace{\mathbb E_{x\sim\mathcal M}\big[\,d_{\mathcal M}\big(x,\;D(\Gamma(E(x)))\big)\,\big]}_{\text{expected mis-communication (distortion)}}$$
$$\text{s.t.}\quad \underbrace{\big|d_{\mathcal S}(Ex,Ey)-\alpha\,d_{\mathcal M}(x,y)\big|\le\varepsilon}_{\text{isometry (3a)}}\,,\qquad \underbrace{d_{\mathcal S}(Ex,Ey)\ge\delta\ \ \forall (x,y)\in\mathcal C}_{\text{safety floor (robustness)}}$$ $$\underbrace{\mathbb E\,|E(x)|\le B}_{\text{rate / brevity}}\,,\quad \underbrace{R\circ E\ \text{is }G\text{-equivariant}}_{\text{substrate (4)}}\,,\quad \underbrace{E(x\oplus y)=Ex\cdot Ey}_{\text{compositional (3b)}}\,,\quad \underbrace{d_{\mathcal M}=\text{task pullback}}_{\text{grounded (6)}}$$

Read it plainly: minimise how often meaning fails to arrive, while keeping the form faithful to meaning (isometry), the dangerous pairs far apart (safety), the words short (rate), the code renderable to any sense (substrate), the grammar generative (composition), and the geometry earned from action (grounding). This single line is the project; everything we build is an algorithm for one of its terms, and the 100 ideas are its coordinate descent.

3.Structural theorems & the tensions they force

  1. Rate–distortion (Shannon). For target distortion $\Delta$, the minimal expected length is $B^{*}(\Delta)=R_{\mathcal M}(\Delta)/\log 7$. Variable length by frequency is the Huffman/MDL optimum of the rate term — frequent meanings get short words provably.
  2. Isometry $\perp$ safety (a theorem, not a nuisance). If $x,y$ are semantically close ($d_{\mathcal M}$ small) but safety-critical (e.g. more/stop), isometry forces $d_{\mathcal S}(Ex,Ey)$ small — below one slip. So safety must break isometry on the critical set $\mathcal C$: the optimum is an isometry with punctures — a quasi-isometry on $\mathcal M\setminus \mathcal C$ and a distance-expander on $\mathcal C$. (Hence $\delta$ hard, $\varepsilon$ soft.)
  3. Prefix-free $\perp$ isometry (why we use a boundary). Isometry wants nearby meanings to share prefixes; prefix-freeness forbids any codeword being an ancestor of another. A parent–child meaning pair then cannot both be words → contradiction. Resolution: the free monoid with a separator $\Sigma^{*}$ (unique factorisation via $\sqcup$), not a prefix code. We hit this empirically — the prefix-free assignment infinite-looped; the fix was exactly $\sqcup$.
  4. No free lunch / Pareto. Rate, distortion, isometry, and safety cannot be jointly extremised; the optimum is a Pareto surface. The application picks the point: Maya weights safety $\delta$ and distortion; an agent-interlingua weights rate and isometry. Same surface, different point — the precise sense in which the “vocabulary fork” was one goal, not two.

4.Grounding as a pullback; self-play as its solver

Meaning must be earned, so $\mathcal M$ is not imported. For a cooperative game with shared reward $r$ and optimal partner policy $\pi^{*}$, set $d_{\mathcal M}(x,y)=D_{\mathrm{JS}}\!\big(\pi^{*}(a\mid x)\,\|\,\pi^{*}(a\mid y)\big)$ — two meanings are close iff they induce the same optimal joint action. Lewis signalling guarantees a Nash equilibrium whose convention is a section of $E$; iterated learning (a Markov chain on languages with a compression bottleneck) concentrates on compositional $E$ (Kirby). So self-play is the fixed-point solver for the grounding constraint. ⚑ Conjecture: replacing the GloVe pullback (co-occurrence) with the task pullback strictly increases attainable isometry, because a grounded $\mathcal M$ has lower intrinsic dimension.

5.Substrate-independence = equivariance; cross-mind = universality

Substrate. Let $G$ act on $\mathcal P$ by transposition (octave shift $\cong\mathbb Z$; channel swap; time-scale) and require $R(g\cdot s)=g\cdot R(s)$. Then meaning is the orbit $G\cdot R(Ex)$ and is invariant to the receiver’s perceptual range — the formal content of “for humans, animals, and machines.” The seven ratios transpose to any octave; the structure is the invariant.

Cross-mind. In the category $\mathbf{Mind}$ (objects = minds’ representations, morphisms = structure-preserving maps), a real interlingua is a terminal object: every representation admits a unique isometric morphism to it. Existence is the Platonic-representation hypothesis with a reason — minds solving the same cooperative problems in the same world are all quasi-isometric to the same task pullback $\mathcal M$. Translation between any two minds is composition through the terminal object: one operation, not $O(n^{2})$ dictionaries.

6.The realness principle (the punchline that includes Maya)

Define the conditional description length $K(E\mid\mathcal M)$ — the bits to specify the code given the shared meaning space.

Realness $:=$ small $K(E\mid\mathcal M)$. The best interlingua is the one of (near-)minimal $K(E\mid\mathcal M)$ among codes meeting the constraints of §2.

Why this unifies everything:

  • Small $K(E\mid\mathcal M)$ means a learner who already shares $\mathcal M$ can reconstruct the code almost for free — i.e. learnable cold (goal 1 / the Maya test), the missing information being tiny.
  • Minimal $K(E\mid\mathcal M)$ is achieved by making $E$ maximally determined by $\mathcal M$’s own structure — an isometry (3a) that is compositional (3b: generators + rules compress the map) and equivariant (4: symmetry quotients the description). Isometry, compositionality, and substrate-independence are not separate wishes — they are the ways $K(E\mid\mathcal M)$ gets small.
  • Grounding (6) makes $\mathcal M$ itself shared across minds, so “given $\mathcal M$” is fair. Robustness and safety are the constraints under which the minimisation runs.

⚑ Realness theorem (target). If $E$ is an $\varepsilon$-isometric, compositional, $G$-equivariant code with $K(E\mid\mathcal M)\le\kappa$, then any mind $\eta$-close to $\mathcal M$ decodes with error $\le c(\varepsilon,\eta,\kappa)$ from $O(\kappa)$ in-context examples. “Maya uses it out of the box” is this bound with Maya as one such mind. Fresh LLMs are our estimator of the constant.

8.Where the estimates stand

  • isometry $\rho$: 0.64 at scale (Phase A, straight-through discrete optimisation) — up from 0.23; RVQ 0.35. ($\varepsilon$ shrinking.)
  • grounding: role-vs-distractor 94% / 21% in sim — the task pullback carves cleaner than co-occurrence (support for the §4 conjecture).
  • compositionality: factored code 100% held-out vs emergent 0% — the homomorphism condition is what generalises.
  • safety: dangerous-confusions 142 → 0.5 / 1000 as $\delta$ (gate) rises — the isometry$\perp$safety trade-off as a Pareto curve.
  • rate: length↔frequency $\rho=+0.44$ — Huffman tendency, learned.

9.What the program says to do next

  1. Kill the shadow (§4). Replace the GloVe pullback with the task pullback at scale; the conjecture predicts isometry and local neighbourhoods both improve.
  2. Restore the local isometry term. Add a $k$-nearest-neighbour agreement penalty (global $\rho$ rose to 0.64 but local neighbour-landing lagged — global ≠ local).
  3. Instantiate the grammar (3b). Make $\oplus$ concrete (predication, modification, negation as the $\mathbb F_7$ reversal operator) so $\langle\mathcal G\rangle\supsetneq\mathcal G$ is exhibited.
  4. Measure $\rho$ in the perceptual metric $d_{\mathcal P}$ (§5), not only $d_{\mathcal S}$.
  5. Estimate the realness constant (§6) via fresh-LLM cold-read — the operational Maya test.

The one sentence. Build the code of minimal conditional description length that is an isometric, compositional, $G$-equivariant, error-correcting homomorphism from a task-grounded meaning space — and any mind that shares the world can speak it. That is the best system for all our goals, and it is a single optimisation, not seven.
