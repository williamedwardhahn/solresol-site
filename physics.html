The physics of a real interlingua

Variational principle · symmetry · geometry · scaling

Don't add a metaphor — find the action whose stationary point is the code, the symmetry and its conserved charge, the geometric bound that caps what's achievable, and the scaling laws with measurable exponents. This turns four things we merely observed into things the physics predicts: (i) the isometry ceiling ρ≈0.64 is geometric; (ii) grounding must raise it; (iii) the safety–availability frontier is a thermodynamic isotherm; (iv) compositionality is a phase transition. 1.Least action — the code is a stationary point

Write the system's action as a free energy over encoders:

$$ S[E] \;=\; \underbrace{\mathbb E_x\, d_{\mathcal M}\!\big(x, D(\Gamma(E x))\big)}_{U:\ \text{distortion (energy)}} \;-\; T\,\underbrace{H(E)}_{\text{entropy}} \;+\; \lambda\!\!\sum_{(x,y)\in\mathcal C}\!\!\big[\delta - d_{\mathcal S}(Ex,Ey)\big]_{+} \;+\; \mu\,\big|\,d_{\mathcal S}-\alpha\,d_{\mathcal M}\big|^{2}. $$

The real interlingua is $\delta S/\delta E = 0$. $U$ is internal energy (mis-communication), $H$ is entropy with $T$ a precision temperature, the $\lambda$-term is a hard-core repulsion on critical pairs, the $\mu$-term an elastic isometry stress. Minimising $F=U-TS$ is exactly rate–distortion = Helmholtz free energy, and MDL = the minimum-free-energy code. The objective is thermodynamics, and it hands us a temperature knob (§4).

2.Symmetry & Noether — meaning is the conserved charge

The transposition group $G$ (octave shift $\cong\mathbb Z$, channel swap, time-scale) is a global symmetry of $S[E]$: it is invariant under $s\mapsto g\!\cdot\!s$. By Noether, an invariant symmetry gives a conserved quantity — here that quantity is meaning: what does not change when you transpose octave, swap colour for tone, or slow the tempo. A rendering is a gauge choice; translation between minds is a gauge transformation; the interlingua is the gauge-invariant content. Consequence: never optimise a rendering-specific quantity — optimise the invariant (measure ρ in the $G$-quotient of perceptual space, not one fixed channel).

3.Geometry — curvature bounds isometry (the ceiling)

$\mathcal M$ is a curved manifold of intrinsic dimension $d_{\mathcal M}$; the code lives on the flat torus $\mathbb T^{L}$. You cannot isometrically embed a curved, high-dimensional manifold into a flat, low-dimensional one — Gauss's Theorema Egregium, the Nash embedding theorem, and Johnson–Lindenstrauss ($L\gtrsim \log N/\epsilon^{2}$) all agree.

The isometry ceiling is geometric, not a training failure. $\rho_{\max}$ is set by the mismatch between $d_{\mathcal M}$ (and curvature) and $L$. ρ = 0.64 is approaching a ceiling, not failing to optimise.

4.Thermodynamics — the safety frontier is an isotherm

Decoding is a Boltzmann distribution $P(x\mid s)\propto e^{-d_{\mathcal M}(x,\hat x(s))/T}$; the ask-gate threshold is a temperature $T$. Our measured safety curve (dangerous-confusions vs ask-again, 142→0.5 per 1000) is a thermodynamic frontier: lowering $T$ freezes out errors but raises abstention — trading energy for entropy. P3: re-plot on information axes and it should trace the rate–distortion $R(\Delta)$ of the confusion channel; Landauer sets a floor on how cheap safety can be.

5.Frustration — isometry ⟂ safety is a spin glass

Critical near-synonyms (more/stop, hurt/help) are pairs isometry wants close and safety wants far — competing bonds with no joint solution: geometric frustration, a spin glass. So the ground state is degenerate (the Pareto manifold), and there is a frustration density $\phi$. P4: vary $\phi$ and measure achievable $d_{\min}$ vs global ρ; ⚑ predict a sharp knee at $\phi_c$ — the maximum critical vocabulary a given $L$ can protect.

6.Renormalization & universality — why minds converge

Abstraction is coarse-graining; RVQ's levels are literally an RG flow. Under the coupling of a shared task, different minds' representations flow to the same fixed point — a universality class. That is the terminal object / Platonic-representation hypothesis, with a physicist's why: shared tasks are a relevant coupling driving representations to one RG fixed point, independent of architecture. Predicts our 98% cross-mind transfer. P5 ⚑: the fixed-point geometry ($d_{\mathcal M}$, ρ) is architecture-independent given the same task (grow the code with GRU/MLP/LSTM; predict the same values within noise).

7.Phase transition — compositionality is an ordered phase

Below a critical world size / bottleneck the emergent code is holistic (disordered — M0/M1 held-out 0%); above it, or with a factored constraint, compositional (ordered — 100%). A phase transition, order parameter = topographic similarity (or held-out compositional accuracy). P6: sweep world size $N$ (or bottleneck width) in world_grid.py; the order parameter should rise sharply at a critical $N_c$; ⚑ below $N_c$ no training yields composition (matching M0's stubborn 0%).
