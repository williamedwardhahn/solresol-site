Solresol

A language of light and sound — that listens, remembers, and teaches by being beautiful.

In 1827 the composer François Sudre invented a language made of just seven syllables: do re mi fa sol la si. Each one is also a note, a colour and a number, so the same word can be sung, painted, typed or played. This is Solresol, rebuilt for the browser. It has 3,152 words, can hear you and remembers what you learn.
